# Jensen Huang at Stanford (2011): The Only Business Lessons You Need
# 黄仁勋斯坦福演讲（2011）：你真正需要的商业课

---

## English Version

In this candid 2011 Stanford talk, NVIDIA founder Jensen Huang distills 16 years of company-building into raw, hard-won wisdom. He reframes "vision" as "perspective" — something everyone has — and traces how NVIDIA's bet on 3D graphics for gaming (a "zero-billion-dollar market" at the time) beat 200+ competitors to become the last graphics company standing. His core lessons: understand the essence of your business before listening to customers, cannibalize your own products before someone else does, cultivate intellectual honesty to fail fast, and never start a company for money — do it because you can't imagine doing anything else. The talk is a masterclass in conviction, reinvention, and the courage to ignore conventional wisdom.

## 中文版

在这场2011年斯坦福大学的坦诚对话中，NVIDIA创始人黄仁勋将16年的创业经验浓缩为最真实的商业智慧。他将"愿景"重新定义为"视角"——每个人都可以拥有。他讲述了NVIDIA如何在一个当时"零市场"的3D游戏图形领域押注，击败200多家竞争对手，成为全球唯一存活的图形公司。核心教训：在听取客户意见之前先理解你生意的本质，在别人颠覆你之前先颠覆自己，培养"知识诚实"以快速试错，永远不要为了钱去创业——只因为你无法想象做其他任何事。这场演讲是关于信念、再造和逆常识勇气的大师课。
