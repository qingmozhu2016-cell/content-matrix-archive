# 5 Social Media Gold Quotes / 5条社交媒体金句
Source: Jensen Huang — "Stanford ASES Summit 2011"

---

### Quote 1 / 金句一

🇬🇧 "I like to use the word 'perspective' because it makes it possible for anyone to have one. When you say 'vision,' it feels like only a few selected visionaries of the world can have one, but everyone has a perspective."

🇨🇳 "我更喜欢用'视角'这个词，因为它让每个人都有可能拥有一个。当你说'愿景'时，感觉只有少数被选中的世界级远见者才能拥有，但每个人都有自己的视角。"

---

### Quote 2 / 金句二

🇬🇧 "If you don't cannibalize your own products, someone will, and it surely will be cannibalized. If you wanna be a market leader, you have to take the initiative to cannibalize your own products and have your ideas cannibalize your own ideas."

🇨🇳 "如果你不自我颠覆，别人就会来颠覆你，这是必然的。想要成为市场领导者，你必须主动蚕食自己的产品，让你的新想法淘汰你的旧想法。"

---

### Quote 3 / 金句三

🇬🇧 "If you want to be successful, I would encourage you to grow a tolerance for failure. But if you fail often enough, you actually might become a failure — and that's different. The key is: fail quickly and change courses as soon as you know it's a dead end."

🇨🇳 "如果你想成功，我建议你培养对失败的容忍力。但如果你失败太多次，你可能真的变成一个失败者——那就不一样了。关键是：快速失败，一旦发现是死胡同就立刻调转方向。"

---

### Quote 4 / 金句四

🇬🇧 "As the CEO, you're either making money, saving money, or raising money. That's the definition of a startup — an enterprise that is nearly out of business all the time."

🇨🇳 "作为CEO，你要么在赚钱，要么在省钱，要么在融资。这就是创业公司的定义——一个随时都快要倒闭的企业。"

---

### Quote 5 / 金句五

🇬🇧 "Money is the only singular reason NOT to start the company. You should start a company because you so believe in your idea, you're so passionate about it, and you wanna build something great. Ideas are a dime a dozen — you have to have a perspective that's unique and be willing to persevere almost any challenge."

🇨🇳 "钱是唯一一个不该成为创业理由的东西。你创业应该是因为你深信自己的想法，对它充满热情，想要打造伟大的事业。想法不值钱——你必须拥有独特的视角，并愿意克服几乎任何挑战。"
