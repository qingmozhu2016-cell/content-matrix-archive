# 5 Social Media Gold Quotes / 5条社交媒体金句
Source: Google DeepMind Podcast — "Inside NotebookLM | Raiza Martin and Steven Johnson"

---

### Quote 1 / 金句一

🇬🇧 "You couldn't Command + F for interestingness. Computers could never do that before. But language models can, because their basic circuitry is prediction — and interestingness is controlled surprise." — Steven Johnson

🇨🇳 「你过去无法用 Ctrl+F 搜索'有趣'。计算机以前做不到这件事。但语言模型可以，因为它们的底层逻辑就是预测——而'有趣'本质上是一种可控的惊喜。」—— Steven Johnson

---

### Quote 2 / 金句二

🇬🇧 "Nobody wants to listen to two robots talk to each other. That will fail and be unlistenable after 30 seconds. You have to master all these very subtle, weird things that people do in conversation for it to work." — Steven Johnson

🇨🇳 「没人想听两个机器人互相对话。那种东西30秒就会让人关掉。你必须掌握人类对话中那些微妙而奇特的细节，它才能成立。」—— Steven Johnson

---

### Quote 3 / 金句三

🇬🇧 "If it can make my PhD thesis sound interesting, then this has the potential to be quite a powerful tool." — Hannah Fry

🇨🇳 「如果它能让我的博士论文听起来引人入胜，那它就有潜力成为一个非常强大的工具。」—— Hannah Fry

---

### Quote 4 / 金句四

🇬🇧 "That's the crazy reality of the language model age: all these things that used to be just about getting the programming right now become more about the rhetoric of it all." — Steven Johnson

🇨🇳 「这就是语言模型时代的疯狂现实：过去那些只需要'把代码写对'的事情，现在更多是关于'如何讲好一个故事'。」—— Steven Johnson

---

### Quote 5 / 金句五

🇬🇧 "No one ever thought about making a podcast based on a family trip to Alaska, because it didn't make sense to rent a studio. But now you can just take everybody's journal entries and photos and upload it to NotebookLM. There's this vast, uncharted territory we've just started to explore." — Steven Johnson

🇨🇳 「从来没人想过为一次阿拉斯加家庭旅行做播客，因为租录音棚完全不划算。但现在你只需要把每个人的日记和照片上传到 NotebookLM。这是一片广阔的、我们才刚开始探索的未知领域。」—— Steven Johnson
