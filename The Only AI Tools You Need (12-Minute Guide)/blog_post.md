# The Only AI Tools You Need — A Mental Model for Choosing the Right One
# 你只需要这几个AI工具——选对工具的思维框架

---

## English Version

After three years of daily AI use at Google and as a creator, Jeff Su distills 10 tools into a clear decision framework. ChatGPT excels at obedience — it follows complex, multi-step instructions without dropping a single requirement. Gemini dominates multimodality, natively processing video, audio, images and text within a massive 1M-token context window. Claude produces the highest-quality first drafts, especially in code and polished copy. For specialist tasks, Perplexity is the "search scalpel" that fetches accurate facts in seconds, while NotebookLM eliminates hallucinations by answering only from your uploaded sources. The key insight: use ChatGPT/Gemini for ideation and research at the start, then hand off to Claude for the final mile.

Source: [Jeff Su — YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=htZRCE2GgIs)

## 中文版

前Google员工、创作者Jeff Su用三年日常使用经验，将10个AI工具提炼为一套清晰的决策框架。ChatGPT胜在"服从性"——面对复杂多步骤指令不会漏掉任何要求；Gemini胜在多模态，能原生处理视频、音频、图片和文字，百万token上下文窗口碾压同行；Claude胜在"首次输出质量"，写代码和润色文案一次到位。专业工具方面，Perplexity是精准快速的"搜索手术刀"，NotebookLM则通过限定源文档实现零幻觉。核心工作流：用ChatGPT/Gemini开头做构思和研究，用Claude收尾做最终输出。
