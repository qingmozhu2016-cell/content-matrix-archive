# How to Use NotebookLM Better than 99% of People
# 如何比99%的人更会用 NotebookLM

---

## English Version

NotebookLM remains Google's most underrated free AI tool. This 27-minute masterclass by Futurepedia covers the full workflow: uploading diverse sources (PDFs, URLs, YouTube links), vetting them with three dedicated prompts that catch contradictions, blind spots, and missing viewpoints, then transforming everything through the Studio Panel — mind maps, infographics via Nanobana Pro, slide decks with custom aesthetics like cyberpunk, AI-generated podcasts with interactive mode, and even full video overviews. The real power move: connecting notebooks to Gemini Gems to build persistent AI assistants — a YouTube strategist, fitness coach, or competitor analyst — grounded in your own trusted data. Free plan gives 100 notebooks × 50 sources; Pro ($20/mo) only adds Studio speed.

Source: [Futurepedia — YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OdCmZvPdr4s)
