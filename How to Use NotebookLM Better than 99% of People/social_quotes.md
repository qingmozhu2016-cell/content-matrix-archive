# 5 Social Media Gold Quotes / 5条社交媒体金句
Source: Matt Wolfe — "How to Use NotebookLM Better than 99% of People"

---

### Quote 1 / 金句一

🇬🇧 "NotebookLM forces citations by default, so everything is grounded in real information. This massively reduces hallucinations — which is one of the main reasons I recommend it so strongly."

🇨🇳 "NotebookLM 默认强制引用来源，所有回答都基于真实信息。这大幅减少了幻觉——这也是我强烈推荐它的首要原因。"

---

### Quote 2 / 金句二

🇬🇧 "I don't build my YouTube strategist in NotebookLM itself — I build it as a Gem connected to my NotebookLM notebook. Now it has custom instructions on how to act, plus access to all my analytics and top-performing scripts."

🇨🇳 "我不在 NotebookLM 里直接做 YouTube 策略师——我把它做成连接到 NotebookLM 笔记本的 Gem。这样它既有定制指令，又能调用我所有的数据分析和爆款脚本。"

### Quote 3 / 金句三

🇬🇧 "Before I dive deep into any research, I run three prompts: find contradictions, surface blind spots, and uncover contrarian viewpoints. If your sources are biased, NotebookLM can't save you."

🇨🇳 "每次深入研究前，我会跑三个提示词：找矛盾、查盲区、挖反面观点。如果来源本身有偏见，NotebookLM 再强也救不了你。"

### Quote 4 / 金句四

🇬🇧 "The infographic feature is my favorite. Nanobana Pro creates stunning visuals — I asked for a comic book villain origin story about AI water usage and got 'Rise of the Hydrovore' with zero spelling errors."

🇨🇳 "信息图功能是我的最爱。Nanobana Pro 生成的视觉效果惊人——我要求用漫画反派起源故事风格展示 AI 用水量，得到了《水魔兽崛起》，文字零错误。"

### Quote 5 / 金句五
